About

We’re the team
that cracks the case
of brand identity.

For two decades we’ve partnered with brands of every size to make merch that people keep, wear and talk about. No middlemen, no mystery fees, no missed deadlines — just remarkable work.

Small enough to care, big enough to deliver.

We started Identify Yourself on a simple premise: promotional merchandise was treated like a throwaway line item, and it deserved better. Better taste, better craft, better service.

Today we’re a team of sourcing veterans, in-house designers, production managers and fulfillment pros — running programs for category leaders and growing challengers alike. We keep our roster curated on purpose, because every client deserves a team that picks up the phone.

Straight-talking partners. Relentlessly curious.

The merch industry has a reputation for opaque pricing and mediocre products. We’re allergic to both. Expect direct quotes, real samples, and a producer who’ll tell you when a cheaper option is better — and when it isn’t.

We love the process as much as the product: chasing down a mill in Portugal, testing a new print technique, obsessing over the weight of a notebook cover. Our best ideas come from that curiosity.
